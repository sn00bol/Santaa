Default item:
Bucket: Wood Bucket, Your hand
Fishing rod: old Fishing Rod, Your hand (again)
bait: Your finger (fr)

These item are default, you could change if you want

SPECIFIC TYPE:
capacity: amount of fish that bucket could hold
durability: durability of fishing rod
skill: The fishing rod skill (ex: SharkRod increased rate to get megalodon or any shark)
bait: increase rarity percent
