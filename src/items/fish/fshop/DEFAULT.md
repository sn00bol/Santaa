Default item:
Bucket: Wood Bucket
Fishing rod: old Fishing Rod
bait: 5 worm

Fallback item if user broke their fishing rod or bait
Fishing rod: Your hand (hand.js in fishingRod)
bait: Your finger (finger.js in bait)
Optional: Bucket: Your hand (HandBucket.js) (bucket cannot be broke)
3 of them cannot sell, trade, and not visible at fish shop

These item are default, you could change if you want

SPECIFIC TYPE:
capacity: amount of fish that bucket could hold
durability: durability of fishing rod
skill: The fishing rod skill (ex: SharkRod increased rate to get megalodon or any shark or dynamite get a lot of fish but worse rarity)
bait: increase rarity percent
